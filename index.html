A quarter-century of Europe's elite leagues — England, Spain, Italy, Germany and France — through 46,709 matches (2000/01–2025/26). Are these leagues converging or drifting apart? Is home advantage really dying? Who referees hardest, and who runs away with the title? Explore it below — every league is linked across every chart.

Is home advantage dying?

Research question 1. For a century, playing at home was worth a small fortune. We track the share of matches won by the home side, season by season — and test what happened when stadiums emptied during COVID-19.

Home-win rate by season

Share of all league matches won by the home team. Shaded band = the 2020/21 season, played almost entirely behind closed doors.

Then vs now: the fall of fortress advantage

Home-win rate in 2000/01 (left) versus 2025/26 (right). Every league slopes down.

Result mix in a single season

Home win / draw / away win split. Drag the slider to any season.

Goals & style: converging or diverging?

Research question 2. Are the Big Five drifting toward one globalised style, or holding their own identities? We follow scoring, shot volume and finishing efficiency.

Volume vs reward

Every point is one league-season (2005/26): shots per game (x) against goals per game (y). Up-and-left = clinical; down-and-right = wasteful. Bubble size = shots on target.

Finishing efficiency

Goals scored per 100 shots. Higher = more clinical leagues.

Whistles, fouls & cards

Research question 3. Southern Europe has a reputation for theatre and yellow cards; the north for letting play flow. Do the numbers agree? We separate how physical a league is (fouls) from how strictly it is refereed (cards per foul).

The temperament map

Fouls per game (x) vs cards per game (y), one point per league-season. Leagues high on cards but low on fouls are refereed strictly; the dashed line is the Big-Five average card-per-foul rate.

Referee leniency

Average number of fouls committed per yellow card. Higher bar = more lenient whistle (fouls tolerated before booking).

Title races: contest or coronation?

Research question 4. Some leagues are genuine sprints to the line; others are annual coronations. We measure the winning margin and let you drill into any final table.

Winning margin over the runner-up

Points separating the champion from 2nd place, by season. Peaks = one-team seasons; dips near zero = races decided on the final day.

Who actually won

League titles 2000/01–2025/26. A handful of clubs dominate an entire continent.

Data & tools. Source: github.com/datasets/football-datasets (match-level results & stats for the Premier League, La Liga, Serie A, Bundesliga and Ligue 1). Pre-processing in Python (pandas): 165 season files merged, standings recomputed from results, metrics normalised per match. Visualisation built from scratch with D3.js v7 (SVG), vanilla JS and CSS. Colour palette is colour-blind-safe (Okabe–Ito derived). Detailed match stats (shots, fouls, cards) are available from 2000/01 for England & Germany and from 2005/06–2007/08 for Spain, Italy & France; lines begin where reliable data starts.
